The founder

Krisin Kumar
Founder, Krisin Clothing

Krisin Clothing started with a simple frustration: most everyday wardrobes were either too plain to feel considered, or too loud to actually wear every day. Krisin set out to build the middle ground: clothing cut cleanly, made well, and designed to be worn on repeat.

What began as a small run of t-shirts has grown into a full edit of essentials: shirts, pants, hoodies, glasses and accessories, each piece held to the same standard: does it earn its place in a minimal wardrobe?

How we work

Fewer pieces.
Made better.

We don't chase trends or release for the sake of releasing. Every piece goes through multiple fabric and fit trials before it reaches the shop, and stays in the edit only if it holds up to daily wear, wash after wash.

No excess packaging, no filler collections. Just clean design, honest pricing, and clothing that gets better with age.
